🎙️ Voice Studio

Turn text and documents into narrated audio

Private server: Speech is generated on your own server and never leaves it. Background music is optional and is mixed in your browser.
Generate waits for the whole narration, then gives you a finished file. Slower to start, but you can mix in background music and download the result.
How much text? Up to 5,000 characters (letters and spaces, not words) — roughly 950 words, about 6 to 7 minutes of speech, or around 2 pages. The Stream tab allows 10,000 characters. Longer text has to be split into parts.
